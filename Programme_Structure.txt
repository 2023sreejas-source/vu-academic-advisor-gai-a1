BMS (Hons) - Digital Business Programme Structure
Source: Vidyashilp University official website

Duration: 4 years, 8 semesters, 164 total credits.

University Core courses: Problem Solving for Design Thinking, Analytical Academic Writing,
Critical Thinking, Humanistic Leadership Development, Communication Skills, Human Values and
Ethics, Learning to Learn, Environmental Studies and Sustainability, Fundamentals of Indian
Constitution and Human Rights, Community Engagement, Socio-Cultural Perspectives on Indian Life.

Program Foundation courses: AI for Decision Making, Introduction to Psychology, Visualisation
Data for Storytelling, Programming in Python, Introduction to Digital World, Language.

BMS Major Courses: Microeconomics I, Probability and Statistics, Organisation Behaviour,
Macroeconomics I, People and Talent Management, Marketing Management, Financial and Management
Accounting, Business Research Methods, Operations Management, Operations Research, Digital and
E-commerce Marketing, Introduction to Digital Business, Financial Management, Fintech, Digital
Supply Chains, Financial Institutions Markets and Regulations, Integrated Marketing
Communications, Strategy and Decision Making, Leading Digital Business, Legal Aspects in Digital
Business, Interdisciplinary Project.

Specialization tracks (choose one): Marketing, Finance, Startup Management, HR Management.

Minors offered: Data Science, Design, Economics, Law, Psychology.
